LolaMarket — to'qima materiallar uchun onlayn savdo platformasi (lolamarket.uz sayti va Telegram ilovasi). Ushbu hujjat platformadan foydalanganingizda qanday ma'lumot yig'ilishi, u nimaga ishlatilishi va qanday himoyalanishini tushuntiradi.

1. Qanday ma'lumot yig'amiz

  • Telegram profil ma'lumotlari — kirishda Telegram taqdim etadigan identifikator va ism. Kimlik faqat Telegram'ning o'z imzolangan kanallari orqali aniqlanadi.
  • Telefon raqami — faqat botda uni o'zingiz ulashsangiz; buyurtma bo'yicha bog'lanish uchun ishlatiladi.
  • Buyurtma ma'lumotlari — buyurtmalar, tanlangan topshirish punkti, izohlar, sharhlar va bahslar.
  • Foydalanish statistikasi — tizimga kirgan foydalanuvchining platforma ichidagi asosiy harakatlari (masalan, mahsulot ko'rish, savatga qo'shish) xizmatni yaxshilash uchun yoziladi.
  • Anonim tashrif statistikasi — sayt tashriflari shaxsni aniqlamaydigan ko'rinishda sanaladi: xom IP manzil statistika bazasida saqlanmaydi, belgi har kuni yangilanadi va odamni kunlar bo'ylab kuzatib bo'lmaydi.

2. Nimani yig'maymiz

  • Bank kartasi ma'lumotlari — platforma karta raqamlarini qabul qilmaydi va saqlamaydi.
  • Parollar — hisobda parol yo'q; kirish Telegram orqali amalga oshadi.

3. Ma'lumot nimaga ishlatiladi

  • Buyurtmani bajarish — sotuvchi buyurtmani yetkazish uchun xaridorning ismi, telefoni va tanlangan punktini ko'radi.
  • Xabarnomalar — buyurtma holati haqidagi xabarlar Telegram orqali yuboriladi.
  • Xizmatni yaxshilash — umumlashtirilgan statistika asosida.

Ma'lumotlaringiz uchinchi shaxslarga sotilmaydi va reklama maqsadida uzatilmaydi.

4. Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

  • Telegram — kirish (autentifikatsiya) va xabarnomalar.
  • Cloudflare — sayt himoyasi, tezlashtirish (CDN) va anonim veb-analitika.

  • Almashinuv HTTPS orqali shifrlanadi; texnik server jurnallari xavfsizlik va nosozliklarni aniqlash uchun cheklangan muddat saqlanishi mumkin.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Operator: LOLAMARKET GROUP LLC (Tax ID 313296186), Samarkand, Uzbekistan.

Contact: info@lolamarket.uz

LolaMarket is an online marketplace for textile materials (the lolamarket.uz website and the Telegram app). This document explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how it is protected.

1. Data we collect

  • Telegram profile data — the identifier and name provided by Telegram when you sign in. Identity is verified only through Telegram's own signed channels.
  • Phone number — only if you share it with our bot yourself; used for order-related contact.
  • Order data — orders, the pickup point you choose, notes, reviews, and disputes.
  • Usage statistics — key in-app actions of signed-in users (e.g. viewing a product, adding to cart) are recorded to improve the service.
  • Anonymous visit statistics — website visits are counted in a non-identifying form: raw IP addresses are not stored in the statistics database, the identifier rotates daily, and visitors cannot be tracked across days.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • Payment card details — the platform does not accept or store card numbers.
  • Passwords — there are no account passwords; sign-in happens via Telegram.

3. How data is used

  • Order fulfillment — the seller sees the buyer's name, phone, and chosen pickup point to deliver the order.
  • Notifications — order status messages are sent via Telegram.
  • Service improvement — based on aggregated statistics.

Your data is never sold to third parties and is not shared for advertising purposes.

4. Third-party services

  • Telegram — authentication and notifications.
  • Cloudflare — site protection, CDN, and anonymous web analytics.
  • Yandex Maps — pickup point map (when the map is shown on the site).
  • Google (Gemini) — when a seller requests AI processing of a product photo, the image is sent to this service.

5. Cookies and on-device storage

  • Website sessions use a protected (HttpOnly) cookie only; the database stores it as a cryptographic hash.
  • Browser storage (localStorage) is used only for convenience — e.g. language choice and cart state.

6. Retention and security

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS; technical server logs may be kept for a limited time for security and diagnostics.
  • The database is backed up regularly; access to backups is restricted.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by writing to info@lolamarket.uz. Requests are handled within a reasonable time.

8. Children

The platform is intended for business commerce and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, updates will be published on this page and the date above will be revised.
